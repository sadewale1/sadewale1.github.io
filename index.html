About

I'm a PhD candidate at Virginia Tech in Human-Computer Interaction, where my research explores how intelligent systems can perceive, model, and respond to human emotion — especially in immersive and embodied contexts.

My published work includes EmoSay, an AI-driven text-to-emotional-speech system for affective communication in Extended Reality (HCI International 2026), and deep learning approaches to video understanding using encoder-decoder LSTM architectures. I also conduct hands-on wearable biosensing research using Empatica E4 and EmotiBit sensors in pre-operative clinical settings.

Across all my work, I'm interested in the human side of intelligent systems: how people feel during interaction, and how systems can be built to honour that.
